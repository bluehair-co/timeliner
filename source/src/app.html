Timeliner

See where you've been and how you get around, from your Google Timeline. Your file is read on this device and never uploaded.

How to get your Timeline file
  1. On your Android phone open Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline.
  2. Tap Export Timeline data and save Timeline.json.
  3. Load it here. Older Google Takeout files (Records.json, Semantic Location History) and iPhone exports work too, and you can load several at once.

Sharper bike rides from Strava

Strava activities replace the Timeline trips recorded at the same time, so rides, runs and walks follow Strava's GPS track. Activities Timeline missed are added too.

  1. On strava.com open Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account and tap Request your archive.
  2. Strava emails you a ZIP file. Load it here as it is, without unzipping. Single .gpx, .fit or .tcx files work too.
